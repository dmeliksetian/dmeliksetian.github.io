Why quantum technologies need separate timelines

Quantum technologies share components, fabrication methods, control systems, and supply chains, but their engineering maturity differs substantially. Atomic clocks and selected magnetometers already support operational systems. Quantum key distribution is deployed in selected networks, while general entanglement networks remain experimental. Quantum computing has demonstrated increasingly capable devices; turning those demonstrations into useful, scalable fault-tolerant computation is still an engineering objective.

How to read the review

  • Count useful computation. State which qubits are counted, which operations are protected, and what error rate and runtime the complete task requires.
  • Match the evidence to the claim. A research demonstration, a customer pilot, and a commercially advantageous production workflow establish different things.
  • Treat roadmaps as targets. Vendor dates describe intended progress; they do not establish that a capability has been delivered.
